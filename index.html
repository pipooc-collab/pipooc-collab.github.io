Android notification filter

Keep the notifications that matter.

silenQo helps you set clear rules for what stays visible and what gets muted. Your notification content is evaluated on your device.

Currently in testing

See how it works Read the privacy policy

What you control

A quieter phone, with rules you choose.

Filter by app, words, and categories. Keep an eye on decisions in a local history that you can turn off or erase.

01

Precise rules

Allow or mute notifications from selected apps, match words, and organize topics into built-in or custom categories.

02

Local intelligence

A bundled multilingual model can help match category rules when simpler rules do not decide. Processing stays on the device.

03

Visible decisions

Optional local history shows what the app allowed, would have muted, or requested to remove. Entries stop appearing after seven days.

Getting started

You stay in control.

  1. Review accessRead the in-app explanation, then choose whether to grant Android notification access.
  2. Set your rulesChoose apps, words, or categories to allow or mute. Turn active filtering on when you are ready.
  3. Review and adjustCheck the optional decision history, edit your rules, withdraw consent, or delete all local data.

Private by design

silenQo does not request Android's INTERNET permission. Notification content, rules, and category learning are processed locally. You can withdraw notification-data consent or delete the app's local data at any time. Read the full privacy policy.
